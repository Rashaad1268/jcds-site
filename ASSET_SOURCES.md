# MUSUBI visual asset sources

The public site uses the club-provided MUSUBI lettering and club logo as its primary identity. External works are used sparingly as contextual, credited editorial material. `scripts/prepare_assets.py` records the non-destructive preparation pipeline; downloaded masters remain in the ignored `.asset-source/` directory.

## Supplied by the club

- Gold `むすび` horizontal lettering
- Gold `むすび` vertical lettering
- Gold `’26` lettering
- Japanese Club of D. S. Senanayake College light and dark logos

## Treatment principles

- Public-domain art remains recognisable and is resized without recolouring.
- Documentary photographs receive only restrained crops, desaturation, contrast, brightness, and web compression.
- Captions identify works and photographers; no external subject is presented as a MUSUBI participant.
- CSS overlays supply the black/gold MUSUBI atmosphere rather than baking artificial effects into source works.
